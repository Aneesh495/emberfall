# Temporal anti-aliasing

The game and editor jitter the perspective projection by an eight-sample Halton sequence in bases 2 and 3. The offsets are measured in framebuffer pixels. Camera culling accepts either the unjittered or jittered frustum. The lighting tiles use the actual jittered projection. The shadow cascade fit uses the unjittered frustum. [Frame recording and extraction](../src/gpu/frame.cpp)

A visible rigid instance with a stable identity supplies its last submitted model matrix. The vertex shader projects the local vertex with that model matrix and the last submitted jittered camera. The fragment shader writes signed UV displacement and two linear view distances to a half-float motion attachment. Scene IDs reserve separate values for related draws such as a mirror and beam. Each rigid body part has its own key. A skinned batch writes its current distance but receives zero history weight because its last deformed positions are unavailable. [Geometry vertex shader](../shaders/scene.vert), [lighting and motion outputs](../shaders/scene.frag), [game extraction](../src/game.cpp)

The resolve fetches the history from the opposite frame slot. It rejects reprojection outside the framebuffer and compares the previous surface's depth with the expected depth at the projected location. The tolerance is the greater of 2.5 cm and 1.5% of the expected depth. The shader clamps previous linear RGB to the current pixel and four neighbors, then reduces history weight from 0.88 to 0.30 as screen-space velocity increases. Fast shading changes and surfaces at nearly identical depth can still smear. The background and skinned cloth use their current sample. The overlay is blended after the resolve. [Resolve shader](../shaders/temporal.frag)

The renderer discards history on resize, antialiasing disable, explicit scene change, camera displacement of at least three units, a forward-vector dot product at or below 0.90, a field-of-view change of at least 0.015 radians, or a far-plane change. Newly visible keys have no motion. The first frame binds current HDR as its fallback sampler so no descriptor points at an unwritten image. The queue barriers move each history image from color output to sampled reads and back to color output on the next use of its slot. The CPU retires a slot's writes by waiting for its timeline value before reusing uploads. [Frame graph](../src/gpu/frame.cpp), [Vulkan synchronization examples](https://docs.vulkan.org/guide/latest/synchronization_examples.html)

The readback fixture compares a first frame to one rendered with the same jitter after an entire cycle. It then changes one object's transform and identity, cuts the camera, recreates the swapchain at a different extent, and switches anti-aliasing off and on. The evidence verifier recomputes the cold/warm difference and compares the cut and reset images byte for byte. The render stress CSV carries a separate temporal timestamp. [GPU fixture](../tests/gpu_temporal.cpp), [evidence verifier](../scripts/verify_evidence.py), [experiment plan](EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md)
